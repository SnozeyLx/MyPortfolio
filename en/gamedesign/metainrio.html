by Richard Mendonça · Game Design project
Virtual experience Altspace Disque Denúncia

Meta in Rio is a virtual environment developed for Altspace in partnership with Disque Denúncia. It was designed to host a podcast by Dora, a digital creator associated with the organization.

The virtual environment provides a meeting place and visual context for the content, welcoming an audience into the conversation.

The concept combines presence, environment, and communication in a social experience. Different views of the scene show the visual identity created for this gathering.

The project explores how a digital space can support content and bring people together around a communication initiative.
