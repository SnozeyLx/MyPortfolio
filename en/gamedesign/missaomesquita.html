  • Missão Mesquita
by Richard Mendonça · Game Design project
Endless runner Adventure Indie

Missão Mesquita is a game built around crossing a lively, colorful city full of possibilities. Players are constantly on the move, combining running, quick reactions, and exploration in a stylized urban setting.

Along the way, players encounter obstacles, characters, and collectibles that shape the game world. Cards and collection screens add a layer of discovery beyond the main route.

The game is designed to be easy to read: players quickly understand that they need to move forward, dodge, and react, while still having room to improve with each attempt. This balance between accessibility and replayability is central to the experience.

Visually, Missão Mesquita blends a recognizable city with colorful characters and interfaces. The result is a distinctive, approachable presentation that feels casual while retaining a sense of progression.

Missão Mesquita was an opportunity to explore how a simple movement system can support a complete experience. The city, obstacles, and cards give players different reasons to keep exploring.

The project also provided a way to test pacing, visual readability, and progression in a browser-playable experience.
