Metaverse Mixed reality Retail experience

WhiteRoom Market is a metaverse retail experience developed with multiple brands. It uses mixed reality to connect a virtual space with a physical environment.

The project explores presenting products and brands in an immersive context, bringing digital presence and physical space into one experience.

The environment combines display and exploration to present brands in a shared virtual space.

The images show different views of WhiteRoom Market and the elements that make up the experience.
