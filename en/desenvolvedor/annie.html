by Richard Mendonça · Development project
Casual game Co-op Project management

Annie's Sweet Nightmares is a cooperative casual game inspired by management games. Players protect Annie’s home using different traps against bears.

The gameplay centers on watching for threats and choosing how to respond. Cooperation helps players coordinate the actions needed to protect the house.

Traps and objects around the house add variety to player decisions and shape the pace of the experience.

The gallery brings together characters, settings, and objects from the game’s world.
